📊 Total

WH = Case × qty per case (helper tab) + Boxes + Damaged + Confirmed, summed over every warehouse shelf. Store = the Daily SOH (LS EOD SOH), shown for reference. Expected = column J of the date tab; Diff = WH − Expected. Rows keep the exact order of the date tab — the export lines up with the sheet row for row. Click a row to see where it was counted.

🕘 History

Transfers, stock added or removed (with reason), opened cases, counting edits and shelf status changes — from this screen and from Warehouse Plan. Add / remove / open case / transfer can be undone here.

🩹 Damaged & Confirmed

Damaged — every line with a Damaged quantity. Confirmed — confirmed units whose variation is a real variation (not Default, not a Case, not “N pc” / “N pack”); confirmed units without a variation are left out.

⚖ Compare with Daily SOH

WH · counted = every counted location except the store, compared with WH EOD SOH of the Daily SOH workbook (latest filled day). The shop is not counted here — its LS EOD SOH is shown for reference only.
