Drag an island to move it — it always lands on whole cells.
Scroll wheel over an island rotates it 45° per notch.

Arrows nudge, R/Shift R rotate, Del remove, Ctrl D duplicate, Ctrl Z undo.
Ctrl + wheel zooms the floor. Hold Alt while dragging a diagonal island for half-cell steps.
